# Bench shorthand (LAU Medical Center-Rizk Hospital micro lab)

Built from real notebook pages. When a new abbreviation turns up, transcribe it raw, list it under Verify, and add it here once the user confirms its meaning.

## Media
- BA: blood agar
- Col: Columbia blood agar
- PV, Choco PV: chocolate PolyViteX agar
- Mac: MacConkey agar
- Chap: Chapman (mannitol salt) agar, the S. aureus screen plate
- Sab: Sabouraud agar, the yeast screen plate
- SS: Salmonella-Shigella agar (stool)
- Schaedler: anaerobic agar

## Growth marks
- ⊖ after a plate: no growth. Two ⊖ = read at 24h and 48h, both negative.
- ⊕: growth or positive test.
- "⊕ on 23/9" on a blood page: the bottle flagged positive that day.
- lac⊖: lactose non-fermenting colonies; lac±: weak or late fermenter.
- "→" followed by a test or plate: the next workup step on that colony.
- "same" after a plate: same growth as the plate above.

## Gram stain
- R.: rare; few; mod; num or numerous.
- PMN, leuco: polymorphs; epith: squamous epithelial cells; no org: no organisms seen.
- GnB: Gram-negative bacilli; GPC or Gpc: Gram-positive cocci (in clusters, in chains).
- "(non motile)" next to a Gram: motility result on the same broth.
- Sputum quality: "<25 PMN/HPF, <10 epith/HPF" style counts are per high-power field.

## Organisms and tests
- α strept: viridans (alpha-haemolytic) streptococci; Nsp: Neisseria species. Together on a throat plate = oral flora.
- β-HS, βHS: beta-haemolytic streptococci.
- Hae: Haemophilus.
- S.a: Staphylococcus aureus; MSSA/MRSA as written.
- BLE: bile esculin; 6.5% NaCl: salt tolerance (enterococcal ID).
- C. Diff Ag: C. difficile antigen (GDH); Toxin A/B: C. difficile toxin.
- Rota, Adeno: stool rotavirus and adenovirus antigens.

## Specimens and sites
- KT: catheter tip (French "cathéter"). ">15 col" is the semi-quantitative roll-plate count.
- bl ①, bl ②, Blood I, Blood II: first and second set. (cent), (art), (Right), (Left): draw site.
- MYC on the label: mycology request.

## Counts
- Urine counts on the Daily Report are colonies per millilitre, whether written col/ml or col/l.

## Printed label, last line
Sample code, sex (M/F), I (inpatient) or O (outpatient), then unit codes (e.g. RICU7, BICU3, RCSU4D). Copy unit codes raw; do not expand them.

## Screening sticker
Six rows: ESBL, CRE, MDRO, AmpC, YEAST, STAPH AUREUS. A handwritten "pos" with NEGATIVE blacked out means that row is positive.

## Unresolved (ask the user, then move up)
- "lac⊖ → s VCE" or "→ s/CE" on rectal-screen MacConkey: meaning unconfirmed.
- "SS lac⊖" on a stool page: lactose-negative colonies present, or none present: unconfirmed.
