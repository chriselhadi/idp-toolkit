Where this comes from

Every morning the 07:00 ID run writes one file, IDP Dashboard YYYY-MM-DD.json, to your Google Drive folder IDP_Data, next to research tables in CSV. This page reads those files from your Drive with your own Google sign-in each time you open it. Patient data is never stored in this page.

Until the first real file exists, the page shows a fictional sample census so you can try it. Checks come from the cross-day verification run: red items blocked the emails until they were fixed or acknowledged with a source.
